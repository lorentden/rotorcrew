Before you order

Know what you need

01

Frame kit only

TPU parts and the battery strap are not included. Please download the TPU files and print yourself.

02

Compatibility

The camera mount uses printable TPU side plates. Files are available for common DJI and analog FPV cameras. Missing your camera? Contact me and I can design a fitting mount.

03

Not included

Electronics, props, motors, camera, VTX, receiver, action cam mount, and LiPo battery are not part of the carbon frame kit.
